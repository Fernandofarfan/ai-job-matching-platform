This policy explains what data {{ operator }} stores in EmpleoIA, why, and what control you have over it.

What we store

  • Account: email, password (only its hash), sign-up date and when you accepted these documents.
  • Your resumes and profiles (encrypted on the server), the application tracker, alerts, search results and generated documents.
  • Security: sign-ins, IP address and browser, to show your account activity and warn you about new sign-ins.
  • Integrations you connect: Telegram, Gmail, the Chrome extension and push notifications.

Artificial intelligence

If you give permission in My account, the text of your resume and of job offers is sent to Google Gemini to generate letters, resumes, scores and answers. Without that permission nothing is sent. You can withdraw it at any time.

Cookies

We only use a session cookie to keep you signed in and another one to remember your language. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention and your rights

Uploaded resumes may be deleted automatically after the period set by the administrator. From My account you can download all your data as a ZIP, withdraw the AI permission or delete the account with all its content.
